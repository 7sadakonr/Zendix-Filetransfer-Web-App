# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Tech-savvy creators, developers, privacy-focused professionals, and everyday users who need to quickly move text or files (up to 100MB) across multiple personal devices (laptops, phones, tablets) without intermediate cloud steps, logins, or registration.

## Product Purpose
Zendix is a peer-to-peer file and clipboard sharing web application that operates directly between devices using WebRTC. It aims to eliminate cloud middle-men, avoid login requirements, and maximize direct P2P speed and absolute privacy while providing crisp, real-time visual feedback (transfer speeds, exact ETAs, and connection types).

## Brand Personality
Tech-forward, high-precision, secure. The voice is clear, helpful, and completely unobtrusive. The design evokes confidence, safety, and instant speed.

## Anti-references
Cluttered, ad-heavy file-sharing websites; slow cloud storage platforms that force account creation and upload steps; over-decorated or slow SaaS landing pages filled with generic neon gradients and irrelevant motion.

## Design Principles
1. **Frictionless Directness**: Remove all barriers to connection and sharing. Zero account creation, zero cloud uploads. Just scan, match, and transfer.
2. **High-Precision Transparency**: Never hide states or progress. Show real-time transfer speeds, precise progress, estimated times (ETA), connection types (LAN vs. Relay), and secure consent prompts clearly.
3. **Task-First Presence**: The interface must disappear into the workflow. Keep layouts clean, interactive response times extremely fast (150-250ms), and typographic scale highly functional.
4. **Intentional Fluidity**: Leverage subtle, premium "liquid glass" background dynamics and micro-animations to reinforce the sensation of fluid data transfer without distracting from utility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast ratios must hit ≥4.5:1 for body and placeholder text on near-black backgrounds.
- High touch target accessibility on mobile layouts (minimum 44x44px buttons).
- Screen-reader-ready markup with semantic HTML and appropriate aria-labels.
- Zero-motion fallback support via media queries (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`).
